Franklin and the Diver
Protect Franklin the turtle. Catch the trash. Save the sea.

About the Game

The ocean is filling with trash, and only you can stop it. Use your net to catch falling debris before it reaches the turtle. Every piece you save helps restore the sea.

How to Play

  • Move your net to catch trash before it hits the turtle.
  • Each saved item increases your score.
  • Miss too many and the turtle becomes endangered.
  • Power-ups and rare items appear as you progress.

Tips

  • Watch for fast-moving trash streams.
  • Prioritize harmful items like plastic rings.
  • Use power-ups wisely to protect the turtle.
